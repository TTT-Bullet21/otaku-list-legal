# Privacy Policy — Otaku List

**Last updated:** 10/08/2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **TTT** ("we", "the developer") collects, uses, and protects information related to the use of the **Otaku List** mobile application (package: `com.aistudio.otakulist.rxq`).

This Policy should be read together with our [Terms and Conditions], which govern the general use of the App.

---

## 1. Data controller

- **Developer/Controller:** TTT
- **Contact email:** ttt.20205ttt@gmail.com

## 2. What data we collect

### 2.1 Data you provide directly
- **MyAnimeList account (optional):** if you link your MAL account, we access your username and your anime/manga list, according to the permissions you grant during the MyAnimeList Co., Ltd. authentication (OAuth) process.
- **Comments and bug reports:** the text you submit through the App's feedback feature.
- **Age range:** the age confirmation you enter during initial onboarding.

### 2.2 Automatically collected data
- **Usage data (Firebase Analytics):** in-app interaction events (e.g., card swipes, buttons tapped, usage time, opening sections like "Your Memories").
- **Device identifiers:** an anonymous installation identifier generated by Firebase, device model, operating system, and approximate country (based on IP, not precise GPS location).
- **Advertising identifier:** your Android device's Advertising ID, used by our advertising networks (Google AdMob and AppLovin MAX) to display ads, including personalized ads if you gave consent.
- **Google Signals data (if enabled):** if you are signed in with your Google account on your device and gave consent to Google, this information may associate your visits with general interest and demographic data that Google already holds. This does not require you to sign in with Google within the App.

### 2.3 Data stored locally on your device
- **Offline sync queue:** changes you make to your anime/manga list without an internet connection (e.g., marking something as completed) are stored privately in your device's internal storage until they sync with MyAnimeList once your connection is restored.
- **Consent preferences:** your decision on the advertising consent banner (GDPR/CCPA) is stored locally to remember your choice.

## 3. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with the following providers, to the extent necessary for the App to function:

| Provider | Purpose | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| **MyAnimeList Co., Ltd.** | Display and sync your anime/manga list | MAL username, changes to your list (if you link your account) |
| **Google (Firebase Analytics)** | App usage statistics | Interaction events, anonymous device identifiers |
| **Google (Google Signals, optional)** | Advanced statistics and advertising | Interest/demographic data associated with your Google account, if you gave consent |
| **Google AdMob / AppLovin MAX** | Display ads within the App | Advertising ID, device technical data |
| **Google Gemini (AI)** | Automatic content translation | Text of synopses/descriptions from MAL (no personal data is sent) |
| **GitHub (via our intermediary server)** | Managing comments and bug reports | The text of your comment/report |
| **Ko-fi / PayPal / Stripe** (if you make a donation) | Voluntary payment processing | Payment data handled entirely by these platforms; we do not access your card information |

## 4. Legal basis and consent

- For users in the European Union, United Kingdom, and regions with similar regulations (GDPR), we request your explicit consent before enabling personalized advertising and data association via Google Signals, through a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform - UMP).
- You may withdraw or change your consent at any time from **Settings > Privacy and Data** within the App.
- For users under 16 years old, certain data collection and personalized advertising features are disabled by default (see Section 7).

## 5. How long we retain your data

- **Analytics data:** retained according to Firebase Analytics' standard configuration (generally up to 14 months, depending on project settings).
- **Feedback comments/reports:** retained indefinitely in our internal tracking repository, until we decide to archive them or you request deletion (see Section 6).
- **Offline sync data:** automatically deleted from the device once successfully synced with MyAnimeList.
- **MAL account link:** retained as long as you keep your account linked; you may revoke it at any time.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example, GDPR in the EU/UK, or CCPA in California), you may have the right to:

- **Access** the data we hold about you.
- **Rectify** inaccurate data.
- **Delete** your data ("right to be forgotten").
- **Object** to the processing of your data for personalized advertising.
- **Request that your information not be shared or sold** (a right applicable in California and similar jurisdictions).
- **Withdraw your consent** at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at **ttt.20205ttt@gmail.com**. Note that some data (such as data processed directly by Google, MyAnimeList, or payment providers) must also be managed directly with those platforms, as they act as independent controllers of that data.

## 7. Minors

- The App requires a minimum age of **16 years** to use (see Section 2 of our Terms and Conditions).
- If you identify as a minor during onboarding, the App will automatically disable personalized advertising and data association via Google Signals.
- We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we have reason to believe we have collected data from a child under 13 without appropriate parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information.

## 8. International data transfers

Since we use providers such as Google, MyAnimeList, GitHub, and AppLovin, your data may be transferred to and processed on servers located outside your country of residence, including the United States. These transfers are carried out under the data protection mechanisms each provider has in place (for example, EU Standard Contractual Clauses, where applicable).

## 9. Data security

- Access to your MyAnimeList list (if linked) is managed through OAuth authentication tokens; we never store your MAL password.
- Sensitive data stored locally (such as session tokens) is stored encrypted on your device.
- Our intermediary servers (Firebase Cloud Functions) manage third-party credentials (such as the token used for the feedback system) securely, without exposing them in the App's code.

## 10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be notified within the App. The "Last updated" date at the top of this document reflects the current version.
