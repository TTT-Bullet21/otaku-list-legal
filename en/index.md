## 1. Service description

Otaku List is an anime and manga discovery application, featuring a swipeable card interface, which uses data provided by the public **MyAnimeList (MAL)** API. In the future, the App may additionally integrate the **AniList** API as a supplementary data source.

The App allows you to:
- Explore anime and manga through swipe gestures, including identification of categories such as Manga, Manhwa, Manhua, Light Novel, and One Shot.
- Link your MyAnimeList account (optional) to sync your personal list.
- Catalog and re-catalog your anime/manga among the available statuses (Watching, Completed, On Hold, Dropped, Plan to Watch) from the "My List" screen.
- Rewatch anime/manga you have already completed, with independent progress tracking, available within the "My List" screen.
- Earn achievements and progress through a level system ("Otaku Level") based on your activity within the App.
- Send comments, suggestions, or bug reports.
- Make voluntary donations to support the App's development.
