## 1. Service description

Otaku List is an anime and manga discovery application, featuring a swipeable card interface, which uses data provided by the public **MyAnimeList (MAL)** API and, optionally, other anime/manga data sources (e.g. AniList, Kitsu, Jikan).

The App allows you to:
- Explore anime and manga through swipe gestures.
- Link your MyAnimeList account (optional) to sync your personal list.
- View your history of completed anime/manga ("Your Memories").
- Send comments, suggestions, or bug reports.
- Make voluntary donations to support the App's development.
